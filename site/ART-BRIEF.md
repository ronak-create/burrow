# Art brief — Burrow landing page

Paste this into the image tool with `blueprint.png` and `public/banner.png` attached.
Generate **one section at a time**, in order. Feed each finished image back in as
reference before generating the next.

## The rules that make the pieces assemble

1. **Canvas is 1920 px wide.** Every image.
2. **Keep the centre 1160 px dark and low-detail.** Roots may cross it, dimly.
   No chambers, no bright lanterns, no focal detail there — website text sits on
   top of it and has to stay readable.
3. **All the good stuff lives in the outer 380 px on each side.** Chambers,
   lanterns, ladders, mushrooms, plants, rock detail.
4. **Top and bottom 120 px of every image: flat dark soil, roots running
   vertically only.** No diagonal crossings at the edges. This is the seam
   between one image and the next, and vertical roots are the only kind that
   line up.

## Style

Match `banner.png` exactly — it is the same world, seen further down.

- Engraved / etched illustration, painted texture, not flat vector
- Violet glowing roots `#8b6dff`, lantern gold `#e8b866`, soil near-black `#0a0712`
- Cross-section of earth: strata, embedded rocks, root networks threading through
- Lantern light is the only warm light, and it is always inside a chamber

## Decide before generating section 01

Whether the chambers are **inhabited**. `banner.png` is uninhabited — lanterns
and furniture, nobody home. Mixing inhabited and empty chambers across sections
will read as a mistake, so pick one and hold it:

- **Empty** — consistent with the existing banner, reads serious
- **Figures** — charming and shareable, moves the brand toward storybook
- **Just left** — chair pushed back, page half-written, lantern still lit. Keeps
  the banner's restraint and still feels lived-in.
